About us

DERSANE HOUSE

A home away from home where students learn, grow and succeed together — guided by respect, discipline and brotherhood.

Who we are

Dersane House is a student house community built on a simple idea: studying is easier, and life is richer, when you live among people who support you. Every member belongs to a house led by an Imam and his assistants, with clear responsibilities, shared programs and a culture of mutual respect.

Succeed

House programs and follow-up keep each member focused, disciplined and accountable.

Our short history

The beginning

A house with a purpose

Dersane House started as a small group of students who chose to live and study together, sharing a room, a routine and a common goal.

Growing together

Structure and leadership

As more students joined, roles were set up — Imam, Assistant Imam, Accountant, program follow-up and cleanliness — so every member knew how to contribute.

Today

An open and organised community

Today we welcome new first-year students, place them in their houses and continue selecting Imams and assistants, with an online process for admissions and announcements.

What we live by

  • Respect — your Imam, your neighbours and each other.
  • Cleanliness — keep the house and every house item safe.
  • Unity — follow house programs and eat meals together.
  • Good manners — no abusive language, no unethical talk.
  • Order — guests only after consulting the Imam.
  • Focus — a calm house where everyone can study.
